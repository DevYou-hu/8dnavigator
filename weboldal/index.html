About us

Built by people who have run the investigations themselves

Quality management support for the medical device industry, focused on nonconformances, complaints, investigations and CAPA. Our mission

8D Navigator is a Budapest-based team of quality professionals with decades of experience in medical device manufacturing, pharmaceuticals and ISO 9001 industries.

We have opened, investigated and closed nonconformances, complaints and CAPAs ourselves, and defended them in audits. We know where investigations stall: vague problem statements, root causes that stop at “human error”, and corrective actions that are never checked for effectiveness.

We started 8D Navigator to share what works. Our templates and trainings are free, so any quality team, from a start-up to an established manufacturer, can run a complaint and CAPA process that is compliant, lean and practical.

The 8D method

Nine disciplines from first signal to closure

8D is the structured problem-solving route we use and teach. Every template and training we offer maps to these steps.

  1. D0

    Prepare

    Assess the event, take emergency actions and decide whether a full 8D is needed.

  2. D1

    Build the team

    A small cross-functional team with product and process knowledge.

  3. D2

    Describe the problem

    Facts, not assumptions: what, where, when and how many, with Is / Is-not.

  4. D3

    Contain

    Protect the customer while you investigate: quarantine, sort, check field stock.

  5. D4

    Find the root causeTrue north

    Occurrence and escape root causes, each verified with data.

  6. D5

    Choose corrective actions

    Actions that remove the root cause, with their risk impact assessed.

  7. D6

    Implement and validate

    Put actions in place and prove they work with effectiveness data.

  8. D7

    Prevent recurrence

    Update procedures, FMEA and training, and apply them to similar products.

  9. D8

    Close and recognize

    Formal closure, lessons learned and recognition of the team.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why are the resources free?

We want good investigation practice to be easy to adopt. Sharing our templates and trainings is how we pass on our experience and get to know quality teams facing the same problems. There is no subscription and no obligation.

Do the templates meet ISO 13485 and FDA requirements?

They are written to meet ISO 13485:2016 and 21 CFR Part 820, and each one references the clauses it covers. Every quality system is different, so review and adapt them to your own processes, product risk class and document control before you release them.

What format are the templates in?

Editable Word and Excel files. You can add your own logo, document number, revision and approval block.

Who are the trainings for?

Quality engineers, complaint handlers, CAPA owners, production and engineering staff who take part in investigations, and managers who review and approve CAPAs.

Can you help with a specific investigation?

Yes. Describe the case briefly in the contact form, without confidential details. We will reply and agree with you how to proceed, including a confidentiality agreement if needed.

Stuck with a CAPA that will not close?

Send us a short description of the problem. We will tell you how we can help.
