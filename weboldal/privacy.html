2. Summary

  • We only collect what you send us through the contact form or by email.

4. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies for visitors and does not use analytics or tracking tools, so we do not show a cookie banner. If we add any non-essential cookies in the future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

5. Recipients and processors

Only the 8D Navigator team can access your messages. We use the following processor, bound by a data processing agreement under GDPR Art. 28:

  • Web hosting and email: [hosting provider name, registered office, website]. Servers located in the European Union.

We disclose personal data to authorities only when the law requires it.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

We do not transfer your personal data outside the European Economic Area.

7. Security

The website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Messages are stored in a protected area of our web server that cannot be reached from the internet, and the administration area is protected by a password. We keep the amount of stored data to the minimum needed.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR Articles 15 to 21 you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict the processing of your data;
  • receive your data in a portable format (data portability);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Send your request to info@8dnavigator.com. We answer within one month. In complex cases we may extend this by two more months, and we will tell you why.

9. Complaints

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, please contact us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hungarian supervisory authority:

  • Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH)
  • Address: 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11, Hungary
  • Postal address: 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9
  • Email: ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu · Website: naih.hu

You can also go to court. The case may be brought before the court of your place of residence.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our data handling changes. The current version is always available on this page, with its effective date at the top.
