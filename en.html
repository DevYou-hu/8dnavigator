For Dad, with love

60 Happy Birthday!

Dear Dad,

For your 60th birthday I wanted to give you something that is truly yours: a renewed website for your knowledge, and a small program on your computer that turns what you say into documents with your name on them.

You have spent your whole career finding what others overlooked. That experience deserves to be written down, in your own words, at your own pace, without anyone in between.

Answer a question when you feel like it. Stop whenever you want. The program will wait for you, and it never sends anything anywhere. Everything stays on your computer.

I wish you good health, many good conversations, and the joy of seeing your knowledge help others. I am proud of you.

With love,
András
