For Dad, with love

60 Happy Birthday!

Dear Dad,

Sixty years. You spent a good part of them finding the mistake where everyone else had already moved on. (We know this quite well at home, too.)

What you learned along the way can't be learned from a book. I think it would be a shame if it stayed only in your head.

So I have two things for you. One is the renewed 8dnavigator.com: still your site, I just tidied it up, and now you can edit it yourself. The other is a small program for your computer. It asks a question, you answer the way you would tell it over a coffee, and in the end it makes a document with your name on it.

You don't have to do it all at once. Answer a question when you feel like it, and stop when it's enough. The program will wait, and it never sends anything anywhere.

Let's sit down together and start it. Ten minutes, I promise 😅

I wish you good health, many good conversations, and that your knowledge helps others too.

I'm proud of you.

With love,
Andris
